# Homepage Content by Role

The homepage should be **one URL** (`/`) that renders differently based on auth state and role. Here's what each version should contain:

---

## 🌐 Guest / Visitor — "Convert them"

The entire goal is one thing: make them sign up or subscribe.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | Bold headline ("Turn Survey Responses into AI-Powered Stories"), short value prop, two CTAs — *Get Started Free* + *Explore Surveys* |
| **Live Stats Bar** | Animated counters — "12,847 surveys published · 2.1M responses collected · 340,000+ active users - AI power survey Insight" (builds trust instantly) |
| **Featured Surveys** | 6 trending published surveys with response counts — guests can browse but see a lock/prompt to vote |
| **How It Works** | 3-step visual — Create Survey → Collect Responses → Generate AI Insight Blog |
| **AI Insight Spotlight** | One featured Insight Blog post (teaser only) with a "Read full analysis — free account required" gate |
| **Pricing Snapshot** | Free vs Surveyor comparison — keep it punchy, 3 key differentiators, link to full pricing page |
| **Footer CTA** | "Ready to start surveying?" — signup button |

---

## 🟢 Registered User — "Keep them engaged"

They're logged in. Show them their world and pull them deeper into the community.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Personalized Greeting** | "Good morning, Rafiq" + a subtle streak/activity indicator |
| **Recommended Surveys** | 4–6 surveys matched to their preference categories, with "New" badges |
| **Continue Where You Left Off** | Surveys they started but didn't submit (if any) |
| **Trending This Week** | Top 3 surveys by response volume this week — social proof |
| **New Insight Blogs** | Feed of recently published Insight Blogs from surveys they participated in — this is the reward loop |
| **Your Activity Summary** | "You've participated in 14 surveys this month" (JetBrains Mono stat) + a subtle encouragement nudge |
| **Upgrade Banner** | Soft, non-intrusive — "Want to create your own surveys? Become a Surveyor →" (surveyor-color, dismissible) |

---

## 🔵 Surveyor — "Their workspace entry point"

This IS their dashboard home. Efficiency over marketing.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Status Header** | Subscription status pill + renewal date + quick "Manage Plan" link |
| **KPI Row** | Total responses (all time), Active surveys, Avg completion rate, New responses (last 7 days) — all in JetBrains Mono |
| **Action Shortcuts** | 3 quick-action cards: *Create New Survey*, *View AI Analytics*, *Write Insight Blog* |
| **Your Active Surveys** | Table/list of their live surveys with response count, status badge, and a sparkline trend |
| **AI Ready to Analyze** | Banner showing surveys with enough responses for AI analysis — "847 responses collected. Ready for insights →" |
| **Recent Blog Activity** | Comments and reactions on their published Insight Blogs |
| **Drafts** | Surveys saved as drafts with a "Pay & Publish" CTA on each |

---

## 🔴 Admin — "Platform health at a glance"

Zero fluff. Pure operational awareness.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Platform Health Row** | Total users, Active surveyors, Surveys published this month, Revenue MTD |
| **Urgent Actions Queue** | Pending reports (count badge) + surveys awaiting approval — anything requiring action floated to top |
| **Moderation Feed** | Last 10 flagged items with priority tags (High / Medium / Low) |
| **New Registrations** | Today's signups with role breakdown chart |
| **Revenue Sparkline** | Last 30-day subscription revenue chart (simple recharts line) |
| **System Notices** | Any failed payments, unusual report spikes, or surveys stuck in pending > 48h |

---

The key principle across all four: **every role's homepage should answer the question *"what do I do next?"* within 3 seconds of landing.** Guests need a reason to join, users need fresh content, surveyors need their workspace, admins need their queue.
