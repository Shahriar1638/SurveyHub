# Dashboard Architecture & Workspaces

The Dashboard serves as the **private operational hub** for Surveyors and Admins. While the Profile is a "Public Storefront," the Dashboard is where the actual work happens.

---

## 🔵 Surveyor Workspace — "The Insight Cockpit"

Focuses on survey lifecycle management, AI analytics, and business growth.

| Section | Content | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational KPIs** | Real-time Performance | Daily response growth, low-completion rate alerts, revenue MTD, and active follower trends. |
| **Survey Management** | Active & Drafts | A list-view with quick actions: `Edit`, `Pause`, `Delete`, or `AI Analyze`. |
| **AI Analytics Lab** | Deep Insight Engine | The primary interface for Gemini-powered sentiment analysis and trend discovery. |
| **Blog Studio** | Content Creation | Drafting and publishing Insight Blogs based on survey data. |
| **AI Settings** | Private Config | Global toggles for `autoAIInsight`, default question formats, and API usage limits. |
| **Feedback Inbox** | Admin & User Messages | Direct feedback from Admins on survey approvals and general user inquiries. |

---

## 🔴 Admin Control Center — "The Platform Guardian"

Focuses on ecosystem health, moderation, and community-wide communication.

| Section | Content | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform Health KPI** | Global Metrics | Total active users, revenue distribution, platform uptime, and system load. |
| **Moderation Engine** | Action Queue | A prioritized feed of reported Surveys and Blogs. Resolve, investigating, or taking disciplinary actions. |
| **System Moderation Stats** | Historical Data | Reports Resolved, Surveys Reviewed, Users Moderated, and Total Actions taken across the platform. |
| **Broadcast Control** | Community Alerts | Interface to issue platform-wide announcements, system maintenance notices, or emergency alerts. |
| **Audit Logs** | Accountability | A private, immutable log of all administrative actions with JetBrains Mono timestamps. |
| **Feedback Management** | Support Tickets | Managing the general feedback and issue reports submitted by users through the Support Center. |

---

## 🟢 User Feedback & Support Center

Even regular users have a "Dashboard" view for their interactions with the platform staff.

| Section | Content | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Report Status** | Transparency | Track the progress of surveys or blogs they reported (e.g., "Under Review", "Action Taken"). |
| **Support Tickets** | Communication | View responses from Admins regarding general feedback or issues they've submitted. |
| **Participation Ledger** | Personal History | A detailed history of all surveys taken and rewards earned. |
